App: Dhaba Tycoon: Arcade Idle • Effective Date: November 2026 • Developer: Chai Dhaba Empire

Welcome to Dhaba Tycoon: Arcade Idle. Your privacy is paramount to us. This Privacy Policy outlines what information is collected, how it is used, and your rights when playing our mobile game.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require users to create an account or provide personal identifying information (such as real name, phone number, or physical address) to play the game.

  • Local Save Data: Your game progression, dhaba upgrades, virtual Rupees (₹), Gems (💎), audio preferences, and unlocked stores are stored locally on your device via sandbox storage (SharedPreferences).
  • Device & Usage Data: Standard anonymous hardware information (device model, operating system version, and general interaction events) may be processed by Google Mobile Ads SDK for ad serving and performance diagnostics.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

This application integrates Google AdMob (Google LLC) to display non-intrusive banner ads, rewarded video ads, and occasional interstitial promotions. These third-party services may collect:

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID)
  • IP address for coarse geographical ad targeting

Google’s collection and processing of data is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy. You can learn more by visiting:
Google Privacy & Terms for Advertising.

3. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & Family Policy)

Dhaba Tycoon: Arcade Idle is designed for a general audience of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided personal information to third-party ad networks through the app, please contact us immediately, and we will take steps to ensure appropriate safeguards.

4. In-App Economy & Virtual Currency

All currencies in the game—including Rupees (₹) and Gems (💎)—are strictly fictional virtual gameplay items. They have no real-world monetary value, cannot be redeemed for legal tender, and are strictly non-transferable outside the game application.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your game progress is stored locally on your device:

  • You can reset or delete all your game data at any time directly through the in-game Settings → Reset Game Progress button.
  • Uninstalling the game application will automatically remove all local save files from your device.
