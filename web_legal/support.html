Frequently Asked Questions

☕ How do I earn Gems (💎) in the game?
Gems are exclusively earned by successfully fulfilling massive curbside bulk orders from VVIP Luxury Cars that pull up on the GT Road. Every completed VVIP car order rewards 3 GEMS!
🚗 How often do VVIP Cars arrive?
During normal gameplay, a new luxury car naturally drives up to the curbside every 3 to 4 minutes. You will hear an authentic car horn sound and the camera will highlight the car's arrival!
⚡ What are Rewarded Ad Deals?
Special Deal Pads spawn periodically inside your dhaba. Stepping on the pad or tapping the left gift badge allows you to watch an optional video ad to activate powerful boosts (such as 2x Staff Turbo Speed, Kitchen 2x, Customer Rush, or a Golden VIP Helper).
💾 How is my game saved?
Your game progress automatically saves to your device every 5 seconds and whenever you complete milestones, hire staff, or claim deals. You can safely close the app and resume anytime.
🔄 How do I reset my progress to start fresh?
Open the in-game Settings (⚙️) dialog and tap the Reset Game Progress button at the bottom. Confirming will reset your store to Level 1.
